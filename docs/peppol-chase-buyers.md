# Belgian Peppol Client-Chase — research only

**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Constraint:** research only. Do not email anyone named below. Do not register a Peppol ID. Do not send a Peppol document. Do not claim that any pack, PDF, or worksheet makes a buyer “compliant.”  
**Already named (do not invent extra inboxes):** FIBORO — public `info@fiboro.be` only. Sister-office mail on the same site is not listed here.

This file is a buyer map for a **399 Client-Chase Pack**. It is **not** a Peppol Access Point (AP) catalogue pitch, legal opinion, or FPS Finance endorsement.

Public rule we cite, not a claim we make: since 1 January 2026, Belgian VAT-liable B2B invoices are structured e-invoices (Peppol BIS by default). A PDF by email is not that invoice. Source: [FPS Finance — structured e-invoices compulsory since 2026](https://einvoice.belgium.be/en/article/structured-electronic-invoices-between-companies-are-compulsory-2026) and [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/nieuws/gestructureerde-elektronische-facturen-tussen-ondernemingen-verplicht-vanaf-2026).

You cannot register on Peppol yourself. Registration is only via an approved Peppol service provider (Access Point). Source: [FPS Finance Peppol FAQ Q5](https://einvoice.belgium.be/en/FAQ/general-questions-about-peppol).

Recurring chase problems (from the rows above, not from us):

1. Buyer has a VAT number but is **not** on Peppol → seller still falls back to PDF mail (Wingman; Freelance Office unreadiness).
2. Seller thinks they are “on Peppol” but cannot prove send/receive (ATEXIO).
3. Invoice **rejected** for ID / totals / missing structured fields (B2B Tax; Coda).
4. Receive side never activated (De Kleine Prins VOILA; peppol.nu “registered ≠ receiving processed”).
5. Mixed channels still open (FIBORO paper + mail; DLV farmers).
6. Associations / special VAT schemes unsure if they are even in scope (OPENDOEK; DLV).

## 3. What a 399 Client-Chase Pack should include vs what only an AP can do

A 399 pack is a **chase and handoff kit**: collect buyer facts, look them up in public directories, draft mails the human may send, preview sample XML, and point at official AP/software lists. It is **not** a Peppol service provider. FPS Finance: you cannot register directly; only an approved service provider can put a participant on the network ([FAQ Q5](https://einvoice.belgium.be/en/FAQ/general-questions-about-peppol)).

**No compliance claim.** Filling the pack, paying 399, or downloading a sample UBL file does not mean the buyer can issue or receive a legal Belgian B2B invoice.

### 3.1 Pack should include

| Item | Why it belongs in a chase pack | Official hook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-screen **disclaimer** | State: not an AP; we do not send Peppol; we do not register a Peppol ID; PDF is not the B2B invoice; this pack is not a legal or tax service. | FPS Finance B2B obligation + FAQ Q5 |
| **Buyer intake** (name, KBO/BCE, BTW, address, B2B vs B2C, existing software) | Chase starts with identifiers. Belgian primary Peppol ID is the enterprise number, scheme `0208`. | [FAQ Q4](https://einvoice.belgium.be/en/FAQ/general-questions-about-peppol); BOSA local rule on KBO as primary ID ([BPA](https://bosa.belgium.be/nl/BPA)) |
| **Lookup worksheet** | Human copies `0208:` + 10-digit KBO into the Peppol Directory. Record hit / miss / “directory not mandatory.” Directory miss ≠ proof they are off-network. | https://directory.peppol.eu/public/ ; FAQ Q6 example `0208:0308357159` |
| **B2B / B2C / B2G split** | Pack must not tell someone to Peppol-chase a consumer. B2C PDF-by-mail is still the ordinary channel. B2G has been in force since 1 Mar 2024. | [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/nieuws/gestructureerde-elektronische-facturen-tussen-ondernemingen-verplicht-vanaf-2026); [FPS B2G](https://einvoice.belgium.be/en/article/e-invoicing-government) |

| **AP handoff sheet** | “Pick an AP from OpenPeppol / BOSA. We are not that AP.” Include the two official list URLs from §1.1. | OpenPeppol certified list; https://bosa.belgium.be/nl/BPA |
| **Chase mail templates (not sent by the pack)** | Ask the buyer for KBO; tell them you will invoice via Peppol once an AP is live; ask them to activate receive; do **not** attach a PDF and call it the B2B invoice. ### 3.2 Only an Access Point (certified Peppol service provider) can do

| Capability | Why a 399 pack cannot do it |
| --- | --- |
| Register the participant on Peppol / SMP | FAQ Q5: no direct registration. Needs a signed Service Provider Agreement and live SMP. |
| Issue or host a **live Peppol ID** | ID is created at AP onboarding with KYC / end-user identification ([Is e-invoicing safe?](https://einvoice.belgium.be/en/article/e-invoicing-safe)). |
| **Send or receive** a real UBL invoice or credit note on the four-corner network | Transport, encryption, and routing are the AP’s job (FAQ Q3). Sample XML in a browser is not transmission. |
| Hold OpenPeppol **PKI** production certificates | BOSA onboarding procedure. |
| Appear on the certified-provider list | OpenPeppol + BOSA update that list after tests + signed SPA. |
| Guarantee delivery, delay diagnosis, or interchange receipts | FAQ Q9: delays sit between sending AP, receiving AP, and the end-user software. Pack has no network status. |
| Replace the legal B2B invoice with a PDF, HTML invoice, or reminder | FPS / VLAIO: structured e-invoice over the network (Peppol BIS unless both parties agree another EN 16931 format). |
| Certify, attest, or “make compliant” | FPS Finance will not even certify the software on its own list. A 399 pack must not either. |

FPS Finance publishes a **technical-means** fine scale (Royal Decree 8 July 2025): first offence €1.500, second €3.000, later €5.000, with a three-month gap rule. Source: [specific e-invoicing FAQ](https://einvoice.belgium.be/en/FAQ/specific-questions-about-e-invoicing). Cite it as public text. Do not use it as a threat in pack copy, and do not say the pack avoids the fine.

### 3.3 One-line product boundary

**399 Client-Chase Pack = intake + lookup worksheet + unofficial chase drafts + sample XML + official-list handoff.**  
**AP = live identity, live send/receive, live SMP, live certificates.**

If a buyer needs the second column, the pack’s job is to **name the official AP lists** and stop.
