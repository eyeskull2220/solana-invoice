**Research only.** Nothing was emailed, DMed, or invoiced from this pass. Public `info@` on the firm’s own site only. No personal Gmail. No guessed local-parts.

**Offer under study:** Inbox-ops pack — intake + invoice + reminder + FAQ — **299 USDC** on Solana, copy-address to `96BT6r5C35cvokdVop8ro4vDtv9zKUxpzrLbXqQbuHk3`. Product files live in PR [#87](https://github.com/eyeskull2220/solana-invoice/pull/87) (`tools/inbox-ops/`). Demo brand is Studio Noord / `hello@studio.example`. This file is the **buyer list**, not the pack.

**Question:** which Belgian small studios, accountants, and coaches — Flanders first, UNIZO-shaped independents — publish a domain `info@` and would reasonably buy that pack for their own inbox?

**Answer:** **14 first-party hits.** All Flanders. Mix: 7 studios, 5 accountants, 2 coaches (plus one studio that is also a vocal coach).

---

## Buyer thesis

UNIZO (Unie van Zelfstandige Ondernemers) is the Flemish association for independents and KMOs. The shops below match that member shape: one-to-few people, Dutch site, public mailbox, live intake and billing over email.

299 USDC is about one billed hour of a Flemish accountant or two coaching sessions of admin. The pack is a one-shot rebrand of four HTML files (intake brief, BTW factuur, overdue letter, client FAQ). It is **not** a Peppol Access Point, **not** a weekly admin seat, **not** Yuki/Exact.

**Where the pack maps:**

| Pack file | Studio | Accountant | Coach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake | New-project brief / offertevraag | New-client dossier + missing docs | Kennismaking / loopbaanvraag |
| Invoice | Deposit + final (21% BTW) | Quarterly fee / one-shot advies | Session / traject / workshop |
| Reminder | Unpaid deposit, late client files | Unpaid honorarium, missing invoices | No-show / unpaid sessie |
| FAQ | Process, timing, what they don’t do | BTW, Peppol, deadlines | Cheques, cancellation, online vs live |

**Honest friction:** these firms pay SEPA, not USDC. Fit here is **workflow**, not “they already hold a Solana wallet.” Conversion still needs a USDC buy or an operator-side EUR-to-USDC step. Since 1 Jan 2026 a B2B PDF-by-mail is not a legal Belgian invoice (Peppol). The pack’s FAQ says so; it does not make them Peppol-compliant. B2C (coaches, some studio work) can still PDF.

## Constraints used

- Flanders first (Oost-Vlaanderen, West-Vlaanderen, Antwerpen, Vlaams-Brabant, Limburg). No Wallonia in the hit list.
- Type: small studio **or** accountant **or** coach. Skip Big-4, SBB, Vandelanotte, PIA/Fidiaz, 30-person shops.
- Mailbox: public `info@` on the **firm’s own HTTPS page**. Directories (boekhoudkantoren.be, Infobel, Belscope) are corroboration only, never the sole source.
- **No Gmail, no Outlook.com, no Skynet, no firstname@telenet.** Domain `info@` only.
- Copy only what the firm already published. No people-search, no KBO director harvest, no guessed `voornaam@`.
- “Would buy” is a **fit call** from public workflow evidence, not a purchase commitment.

## Honest gap

- **14 public `info@` hits, all Flanders, all first-party.** That is enough to start a Dutch outreach list. It is **not** a UNIZO member dump; UNIZO does not publish member inboxes.
- **Studios and coaches are the real buyers.** Accountants have the mailbox and the four jobs, but they already sell digital books. Pitch them last, as a kit for *their* `info@`, not as bookkeeping software.
- **USDC is the conversion cliff.** None of these sites mention crypto. 299 USDC is cheap in euros; paying it on Solana is new. Do not pretend otherwise.
- **Peppol:** do not sell the pack as a legal B2B invoice after 1 Jan 2026. FAQ in [#87](https://github.com/eyeskull2220/solana-invoice/pull/87) already says that.
- **No mail from this PR.** Team delivers the pack; this file is research.

**Do not apply. Do not mail these inboxes from this PR.**
