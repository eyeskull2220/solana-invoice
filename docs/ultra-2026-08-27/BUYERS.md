- Secretaris quote on the homepage: Charles De Roeck, Secretaris Atriac — “Als club zagen we het bos niet meer door de bomen. Dan voel je dat Twizzit een noodzaak wordt.”

- KLJ (opened, §1) already **requires** Twizzit for the secretaris. Do not mail KLJ afdelingen a “Twizzit alternative.”

**Neighbour math (from Twizzit’s own tariff, not a quote):** 120 leden × €3.75 × 3 years = €1,350 excl. btw licence, plus €195 start, before any payment fees. That is why a €199 / €349 / €900 one-shot can sit **next to** Twizzit for clubs that will not sign a 3-year, 100-member floor — and must not be sold as ripping Twizzit out of a club that already lives there.
