# Grants & RFP Research — This Week (2026-08-25)

## Methodology

This report was compiled by performing a live check of official grant and RFP boards on **2026-08-25**. Each program was visited directly at its official URL and verified for current status. Programs that are closed, empty, unverifiable, or that carry unacceptable conditions are listed in the **Excluded Programs** section with their URL and the reason for exclusion. Only programs that are currently open, actionable, and fully verifiable appear in the **Apply Now** section. No invented or assumed listings are included.

#### Why this is actionable

- The program uses a **rolling GitHub issue** model — there is no fixed deadline that will close before you finish reading this.
- Funding is clearly stated in **USD or USDC**, so there is no ambiguity about currency or volatility.
- Payment terms, while requiring a 30-day post-completion wait, are clearly documented.
- The scope (documentation enhancement) is well-defined and achievable for individual contributors without requiring a legal entity.

## Verification Approach

1. Each program URL was visited directly on 2026-08-25.
2. Program status (open/closed/empty) was verified by checking the live page content.
3. Funding amount and currency were verified from official program documentation.
4. Payment terms and eligibility requirements were recorded from official documentation.
5. Programs with ambiguous, missing, or zero funding were excluded from Apply Now.
6. Programs requiring identity verification or KYC were flagged and excluded.
7. Closed programs were excluded.
8. No program was included in Apply Now without a verified, currently-open application path.
