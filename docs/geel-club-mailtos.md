## Rules used

- Public `info@` only, as published on the city listing (or, for KST, on the club’s own public contact page).
- No personal Gmail. Personal ISP mailboxes (`telenet.be`, `skynet.be`, `hotmail`/`live`/`outlook`, and similar) are also omitted from every mailto list.
- **Later-list only:** asvgeel, Valberta, Den Bruul, BBC Geel, t Stat, zwemclubkst. Those six sit in [Later-list only](#later-list-only-do-not-mail) and nowhere else as mail targets.
- Clubs that already have a modern site are flagged **do not mail**.
- Role inboxes that are not `info@` (`secretaris@`, `bestuur@`, `secretariaat@`, …) are not mail targets.

## Method

1. Download the A–Z index at [https://www.geel.be/verenigingen](https://www.geel.be/verenigingen) (Icordis CMS).
2. Open every association detail URL (and the few non-`/verenigingen/detail/` aliases such as `/tStat`, `/Preludo`).
3. Keep only `info@` values published on those pages (hex-encoded `mailto:` on geel.be decoded).
4. Drop `@gmail.com` and other personal mailboxes.
5. HEAD/GET each remaining club domain to classify modern vs dated/dead.
6. Place the six named clubs on the later-list only. Confirm KST from its own public pages, not the Geel gids.

Sport Vlaanderen’s [Sportdatabank](https://www.vlaanderen.be/cultuur-sport-en-vrije-tijd/sport/sportdatabank-vlaanderen) is a possible cross-check; this pass used the municipal listing as the source of truth.
