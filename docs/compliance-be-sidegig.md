# Belgian legal/tax plan — agent-delivered services paid in Solana USDC (Geel)

**Date of this plan:** 2026-08-26  
**Status:** working plan for an operator who is **not yet registered** as a Belgian self-employed person.  
**This is not legal, tax, or social-security advice.** It does not create a KBO number, a VAT number, a Peppol ID, or a social-insurance affiliation. It does not say the operator is compliant today.

Confirm every figure with the cited official page or with a recognised *ondernemingsloket*, social insurance fund, and an ITAA accountant **before** any filing. Amounts below are 2026 snapshot values; funds add their own *beheerskosten* on top of the RSVZ legal base.

## 0. What this plan assumes (and what it does not)

**Assumed facts (operator profile)**

- Lives and works from **Geel** (Flemish Region, province of Antwerp).
- Sells **agent-delivered services** (software / automation / digital delivery) for a fee.
- Receives **USDC on Solana** into a **personal Phantom wallet** (self-custody). Not XRP, not SOL as the payment asset.
- Already has a **half-time or more employee job** (Track A is available if that job still meets the statutory hours test).
- Is **not** a Peppol Access Point and will not become one.

**Hard limits of this file**

- **Plan, not advice.** A recognised accountant / social insurance fund / VAT office must apply the rules to the facts.
- **No invented identifiers.** This file does not contain, and must not be filled with, a KBO number, a VAT number (`BE0…`), or a Peppol participant ID. Those exist only after a real *ondernemingsloket* and FPS Finance (e604) procedure.
- **Not already compliant.** Receiving USDC in a personal Phantom wallet while selling services is **not** a completed Belgian start-up. KBO, social insurance, VAT identification, invoicing, and books are still open work.
- **No personal Gmail or other private contact details.** Official government and fund URLs only.

## 1. Two tracks (read both; pick one before KBO)

## 2. Shared foundation — KBO via an *erkend ondernemingsloket*

Selling services for consideration from a Belgian establishment is a **self-employed professional activity**. A natural person in that position must be registered in the **Kruispuntbank van Ondernemingen (KBO)** **before** invoicing.

- Registration is **only** through one of the eight accredited *ondernemingsloketten*. There is no self-service KBO form at FPS Economy.
- The eight counters (FPS Economy list): **Acerta, Eunomia, Group S, Liantis, Partena, Securex, UCM, Xerius**.
- On registration the counter issues a **10-digit ondernemingsnummer** (first digit 0 or 1). That number is later reused as the VAT number after FPS Finance identification. **Do not invent one in the meantime.**
- **2026 regulated fee:** **€111.50** per establishment unit (index-linked; same at every counter; not subject to VAT). Liantis (Geel office) also quotes **€83.49** if the counter files VAT identification for you; identification via **e604 in MyMinfin is free**.
- **Flanders (Geel):** proof of *basiskennis bedrijfsbeheer* is **not** required (abolished in the Flemish Region since 1 September 2018). Wallonia/Brussels still require it — irrelevant unless the seat moves.
- Ask the counter to propose **NACE** codes for agent-delivered IT/software services (commonly 62.01 / 62.02 / 63.xx — **the counter assigns, this plan does not**).
- Establishment unit: the Geel home/office address from which the activity is exercised.

**Do this before the first paid job**, not after the first USDC lands.

Sources: [FPS Economy — zelfstandige worden](https://economie.fgov.be/nl/themas/ondernemingen/een-onderneming-oprichten/zelfstandige-worden-belgie), [FPS Economy — KBO inschrijving](https://economie.fgov.be/nl/inschrijving-de-kruispuntbank), [FPS Economy — steps at the business counter](https://economie.fgov.be/en/themes/enterprises/starting-business/steps-starting-business/steps-take-business-counter), [office finder](https://weblists.economie.fgov.be/weblists/dataDisplay.xhtml?app=7&lang=NL&list=10). Liantis has a walk-in office at Diestseweg 63, 2440 Geel ([fund list, updated 03/2026](https://socialsecurity.belgium.be/nl/sociaal-beleid-mee-vorm-geven/zelfstandigen/lijst-van-sociale-verzekeringsfondsen)).

## 3. Shared foundation — social insurance fund (*sociaal verzekeringsfonds*)

Affiliation is **mandatory on both tracks**, **before** the activity starts (earliest six months ahead). Late affiliation: RSVZ reminder (30 days), then automatic enrolment in the **Nationale Hulpkas**, plus an administrative fine of **€500–€2,000**.

The fund may be chosen freely. The *ondernemingsloket* can file affiliation in the same sitting. Official 2026 list: [FPS Social Security — lijst fondsen](https://socialsecurity.belgium.be/nl/sociaal-beleid-mee-vorm-geven/zelfstandigen/lijst-van-sociale-verzekeringsfondsen). **Legal 2026 contribution scales** (RSVZ / FPS Social Security, **before** fund *beheerskosten* of roughly 3–4%):

| Band (net taxable self-employed income *I*) | Rate |
|---|---|
| First slice up to **€75,024.54** | **20.50%** |
| Slice **€75,024.55 – €110,562.42** | **14.16%** |

## 4. Shared foundation — BTW *vrijstellingsregeling* (threshold **€25,000**)

Agent-delivered services are in principle **VAT-taxable**. Identification with FPS Finance is still required even under the small-business exemption: the operator remains a taxable person, **with a VAT number**, but does not charge VAT and cannot deduct input VAT.

**2026 threshold still in force:** annual turnover **€25,000 excl. VAT** (art. 56bis VAT Code). A federal intention (April 2026) to raise it to €30,000 is **not law** until art. 56bis is amended and published in the *Belgisch Staatsblad*. Until then, plan against **€25,000**.

Official pages:

- NL: [FOD Financiën — Btw-vrijstellingsregeling](https://financien.belgium.be/nl/ondernemingen/btw/btw-plicht/vrijstellingsregeling)
- EN: [FPS Finance — VAT exemption scheme for small businesses](https://finance.belgium.be/en/enterprises/vat/vat-obligation/vat-exemption-scheme-small-businesses)

**How to opt in 2026:** application **e604A** (start) in MyMinfin. Mid-year start: the €25,000 ceiling is **cut pro rata** by elapsed calendar days since 1 January. FPS Finance example: start 1 July 2026 → reduced ceiling **€12,603**; estimated H2 turnover of €15,000 **blocks** the exemption for 2026.

**10% overrun rule (calendar year):**

- Overrun **≤ 10%** (€27,500): keep the exemption **this year**, lose it on **1 January next year**.
- Overrun **> 10%**: switch to the normal VAT regime **from the transaction that breached**.
- After leaving the scheme, a full calendar year must pass before re-entry (typically 1 January of year N+2).

**What the exemption does *not* remove**

- VAT identification number (the KBO number, once activated).
- Invoicing rules, including the invoice mention **“Bijzondere vrijstellingsregeling kleine ondernemingen”**.
- Annual **customer listing** via Intervat (nihil listing if no B2B Belgian VAT customers). For 2025, FPS Finance extended the nihil listing to **30 April 2026**; thereafter **31 March**.
- **Peppol receive** capability (and **send** to Belgian VAT-registered B2B customers) — see §5.
- Special VAT return for intra-Community acquisitions / reverse-charge services where the operator is the debtor.

**Turnover for the threshold** is the EUR value of supplies (including USDC-paid jobs — see §6). Crypto is not a way around the ceiling.

**When to skip the exemption:** mainly B2B customers who recover VAT, or large input VAT on tools. An accountant should run that comparison **before** e604A.

Construction / certified POS / old-materials trades are excluded; typical software services are not.

## 5. Shared foundation — Peppol B2B (operator is **not** an Access Point)

Since **1 January 2026**, Belgian-established VAT-taxable persons must **issue and receive** structured e-invoices for **domestic B2B** (Belgian VAT-taxable supplier ↔ Belgian VAT-taxable customer). PDF-by-email is **not** a valid B2B invoice in scope.

The mandate **includes** taxable persons on the **€25,000 exemption**.

Official FPS mandate hub: [efactuur.belgium.be](https://efactuur.belgium.be/nl/article/gestructureerde-elektronische-facturen-tussen-ondernemingen-verplicht-sinds-2026) · [who is in scope](https://efactuur.belgium.be/nl/article/voor-wie-wordt-e-facturatie-verplicht) · [B2B FAQ](https://efactuur.belgium.be/nl/FAQ/algemene-vragen-b2b).

| Channel | Structured e-invoice (Peppol BIS / EN 16931) | PDF / paper |
|---|---|---|
| **B2B** (two Belgian VAT-taxable persons) | **Mandatory** via Peppol (unless both parties agree in writing on another EN 16931 format) | Not allowed |
| **B2G** | Mandatory (B2G rules since 1 March 2024; federal buyers also below €3,000) | Not allowed |
| **B2C, extra-EU, other out-of-scope** | Allowed if the recipient agrees (Peppol registration counts as agreement) | Allowed |

**This operator is not a Peppol Access Point.** An Access Point is a **certified network gateway** (OpenPeppol). End-users do **not** host one. The plan is:

1. Pick **end-user invoicing/accounting software** from the FPS Finance list of conforming applications: [softwareoplossingen](https://efactuur.belgium.be/nl/article/softwareoplossingen-voor-het-verzenden-ontvangen-en-verwerken-van-elektronische-facturen).
2. That software connects **through a certified Access Point** (the vendor’s, or one the vendor lets the user choose). Certified providers: [OpenPeppol directory](https://peppol.org/) / FPS list of service providers on the same e-factuur site.
3. After KBO + VAT identification, the Peppol participant ID is typically scheme **`0208:` + enterprise number** (Belgian KBO). **Do not mint a fake ID.**
4. Even with **only private (B2C) buyers**, the operator must still **be able to receive** structured invoices from Belgian suppliers.

**Tax incentive (2024–2027):** 120% cost deduction on e-invoicing subscription/advice for small businesses; 20% investment deduction for qualifying digital assets from 2025. [FPS — fiscale stimulansen](https://efactuur.belgium.be/nl/article/fiscale-stimulansen-voor-het-invoeren-van-e-facturatie).

VLAIO summary (Flanders): [structured e-invoices mandatory](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/nieuws/gestructureerde-elektronische-facturen-tussen-ondernemingen-verplicht-vanaf-2026).

## 6. Shared foundation — book USDC at EUR value **on receipt** as *beroepsinkomen*

Payment for agent-delivered services is **professional income** (*beroepsinkomen*), not private-asset capital gains, not miscellaneous speculation, and not “deeleconomie”. It belongs in the **personenbelasting** with other earned income (employee salary + self-employed profit on Track A).

**Booking rule (plan)**

1. **Raise an invoice first** (EUR amount; USDC accepted as settlement). Under the exemption, state *Bijzondere vrijstellingsregeling kleine ondernemingen*. For Belgian B2B, send it over Peppol.
2. **On-chain receipt date = income date.** Convert the USDC amount to **EUR at that moment** and book that EUR figure as turnover (*verkoopboek*) and as a receipt (*financieel dagboek* — treat the Phantom receiving address as a professional cash/crypto account, not as “unbooked”).
3. **Rate source.** There is no FPS-published official crypto FX table. USDC is USD-pegged, so the defensible professional method is:
   - take the **ECB euro foreign-exchange reference rate for USD** of the receipt date (or the last published TARGET business day if the receipt is on a weekend/holiday);
   - apply **1 USDC ≈ 1 USD**, and **document any deviation** if a market USDC/USD print is not 1.000;
   - store the ECB page or a dated extract with the invoice.
   - ECB rates: [Euro foreign exchange reference rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html).
4. **Evidence pack per job:** invoice number, customer identity, service description, USDC amount, Solana transaction signature, receiving address, ECB USD/EUR rate, EUR booked, date/time.
5. **Later conversion to a Belgian EUR account** (via an exchange or on-ramp) is a **second event**. Book the EUR bank credit. The difference versus the amount booked at receipt is a professional FX gain or loss of that later date — not a chance to leave the original fee unreported.
6. **Do not net “I still hold USDC, so I have no income.”** Holding the token does not defer professional income on a fee already earned.

**Wallet hygiene (still a gap today)**

The current **personal** Phantom wallet mixes private keys and (possibly) private tokens with professional receipts. The plan after KBO is a **dedicated receiving address** (still Phantom/self-custody is fine) used only for invoices, with the same evidence pack. That is bookkeeping hygiene, not a completed registration.

**What this is *not***: the 10% private crypto capital-gains regime (income year 2026) and the 33% miscellaneous-income bucket. Those concern private portfolio management / speculation. Fees for services paid in USDC sit in **Vak IV (beroepsinkomen)** with social contributions on the net profit.

## 7. Shared foundation — CAP for **foreign exchanges**

Belgian tax residents must:

1. **Once:** report each **foreign account** to the **Centraal Aanspreekpunt (CAP)** at the National Bank of Belgium, at the latest with the tax return for the year the account existed.
2. **Every year:** tick the account in the personal income-tax return **Vak XIII, rubriek A**.

Official:

- [FPS Finance — CAP](https://financien.belgium.be/nl/thesaurie/cap)
- [FPS Finance — buitenlandse rekeningen](https://fin.belgium.be/nl/particulieren/internationaal/buitenlandse-inkomsten-rekeningen/rekeningen)
- CAP portal (eID / itsme): [cappcc.nbb.be](https://cappcc.nbb.be)
- NBB how-to: [Buitenlandse rekeningen melden](https://www.nbb.be/nl/kredietcentrales/centraal-aanspreekpunt-van-rekeningen-en-financiele-contracten-cap/melden-0)

**What to report**

- Any account at a **foreign bank, exchange (*wisselinstelling*), credit or savings institution** — including a **custodial crypto account** at Binance, Kraken, Coinbase, Bitstamp, etc., if used to cash out USDC or to hold professional proceeds. FPS Finance explicitly lists *cryptomuntrekeningen* and advises declaring foreign crypto accounts while the precise perimeter evolves.
- Report even if the account existed only for a few days.
- Data per account: account number (or platform UID), name of the institution, BIC **or** full address, country, year opened.

**What a personal Phantom wallet is**

A **self-custodial** wallet is **not** “an account opened at a foreign institution.” CAP is aimed at accounts **held by** a bank/exchange. Do **not** invent a BIC or a foreign-bank file for Phantom. Keep the Solana evidence pack for the *beroepsinkomen* file instead.

**The moment CAP becomes mandatory in this workflow** is the first time USDC is sent to a **custodial foreign exchange** (or a foreign bank/EMI) to convert to EUR. File CAP **before or with** the next tax return; do not wait for a reminder. DAC8/CASP reporting by EU platforms from 2026 is **extra** visibility for FPS Finance — it does **not** replace the resident’s CAP + Vak XIII duties.

PayPal/Google-type wallets: FPS Finance says declare them if they are used **for a professional activity** or if a balance remains longer than strictly needed to settle. (Not a Gmail; use the platform account id if a CAP row is required.)

Sanctions: tax increases and fines for undeclared foreign accounts; automatic exchange (CRS / DAC8) is already the detection path.

## 8. Shared foundation — books, personsbelasting, Geel, and **when to hire an accountant**

**Books.** An *eenmanszaak* under **€500,000** turnover (excl. VAT), outside insurance/credit/investment, may keep a **simplified** set of books: purchase book, sales book, financial journal (cash + “bank”/crypto), inventory book. [VLAIO — voer een heldere boekhouding](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/verplichtingen-na-de-start/voer-een-heldere-boekhouding). A dedicated EUR current account for the activity is still the practical way to feed the financial journal; USDC receipts need an extra column or sub-account.

**Personsbelasting (income year 2026 / assessment year 2027)** — progressive federal rates on aggregated taxable income:

| Slice | Rate |
|---|---|
| €0 – €16,720 | 25% |
| €16,720 – €29,510 | 40% |
| €29,510 – €51,070 | 45% |
| above €51,070 | 50% |

Tax-free allowance **€11,180** (income year 2026). [FPS Finance — belastingtarieven](https://fin.belgium.be/nl/particulieren/belastingaangifte/inkomsten/belastingtarieven).

**Stad Geel** complementary municipal tax on the federal personal income tax: **7.0%** (assessment year 2026 list). [FPS Finance municipal rates PDF](https://fin.belgium.be/sites/default/files/media/documents/tarieven-gemeentebelasting-2026.pdf) · [Vlaanderen — aanslagvoeten per gemeente](https://www.vlaanderen.be/lokaal-bestuur/financiering/opcentiemen-en-aanvullende-lokale-belastingen/aanslagvoeten-per-gemeente). Confirm the 2027 assessment-year rate when the 2027 table is published.

**Employee contract (Track A).** Read the employment contract for exclusivity, IP, and non-compete **before** KBO. The social-security hours test (half-time) does not override a contractual ban.

### When to hire an accountant (ITAA)

A bookkeeper is **not legally required** for a simplified *eenmanszaak*. Hire one **before** e604 / first invoice in any of these cases (this operator already hits several):

| Trigger | Why a professional is in the critical path |
|---|---|
| **Now — crypto settlement** | EUR-on-receipt method, FX on cash-out, mixing a personal Phantom with fees: easy to under- or double-count. |
| **Belgian VAT-registered B2B buyers** | Peppol send + customer listing + exemption wording. |
| **First foreign exchange cash-out** | CAP + Vak XIII + professional FX difference. |
| **Turnover approaching €20k in a calendar year** | €25,000 exemption and the 10% overrun rule; pro-rata if the start is mid-year. |
| **Employee hours might dip below half-time** | Silent reclassification to hoofdberoep = four minima. |
| **Considering Track B** | Primostarter request, cash-flow of ~€3.7k/year minima, VAPZ, bridging right. |
| **Any VAT normal-regime choice** | Periodic returns, input VAT, reverse charge on foreign SaaS. |
| **The operator does not want to maintain the four books** | Software + accountant is cheaper than a VAT or contribution regularisation. |

Look up a recognised practitioner on [ITAA](https://www.itaa.be/). The *ondernemingsloket* / Liantis-Partena starter desk can name local Geel/Kempen accountants; that is a referral, not a substitute for the engagement letter.

**Hire *before* the first paid USDC**, not after the tax-return deadline.

## 9. Track A — *zelfstandige in bijberoep* (keep the employee job)

### Eligibility (check every quarter)

Per [Liantis — starten in bijberoep](https://www.liantis.be/nl/zelfstandig-worden/sociaal-statuut/zelfstandige-in-bijberoep) (same test used by the funds / RSVZ):

- Employee occupation **≥ 50%** on average **per quarter**, **and**
- **≥ 235 hours per quarter** on a 38-hour week.
- Statutory appointed teacher: **≥ 60%** and **≥ 282 hours/quarter**.

Start on a day the half-time contract (or qualifying benefit) still runs through the **end of that quarter**, or the first quarter can be coded hoofdberoep.

Interim / fragmented contracts: have the fund confirm **before** affiliation.

### 2026 contributions (bijberoep)

From the [official 2026 table](https://socialsecurity.belgium.be/sites/default/files/content/docs/nl/sociaal-beleid-vorm-geven/zelfstandigen/bijdragennota_2026_tabel_nl.pdf) and [Liantis bijberoep](https://www.liantis.be/nl/zelfstandig-worden/sociale-bijdragen/bijberoep):

| Net taxable self-employed income (year) | Result |
|---|---|
| **< €1,922.16** | **No contributions** (ask the fund in writing to waive the provisional amount if this is certain). |
| **€1,922.16 – €75,024.54** | **20.50%** (plus fund fees). |
| **€75,024.55 – €110,562.42** | **14.16%** on the upper slice. |
| above **€110,562.42** | ceiling. |

- **No minimum.** Unlike Track B, a quiet year does not still cost ~€900/quarter.
- Starter **provisional** (Liantis): **€102.40**/quarter (= legal floor on €1,922.16 + fees). Raise it if actual profit will be higher, to avoid a large regularisation ~two years later.
- RSVZ legal provisional/minimum line for bijberoep: **€98.51**/quarter before fees.
- If bijberoep contributions reach the **hoofdberoep minimum**, extra self-employed rights (including extra pension) can start to accrue — optional, not automatic at low volume.

Social cover (health, unemployment from the job, employee pension) stays with the **employer scheme**. Bijberoep contributions at low income do **not** replace that.

### Tax

Self-employed **net** profit (turnover in EUR as booked in §6, minus real professional expenses, minus the social contributions actually paid) is added to the employee salary. Marginal rate is often **45% or 50%** plus **7.0%** Geel municipal surcharge on the federal tax. Budget cash for that; there is no withholding on USDC.

### Track A sequence

1. Confirm the hours test for the **current and next** quarter; read the employment contract.
2. Pick a fund + *ondernemingsloket* (same group is administratively simpler).
3. Affiliate **before** start date; register KBO; file e604A for the **vrijstelling** if the pro-rata ceiling allows.
4. Connect Peppol-capable software as a **user** (not as an Access Point).
5. Open a dedicated Solana receiving address; start the evidence pack; never invoice without a KBO/VAT number once issued.
6. If a foreign exchange is used to convert USDC → EUR, file **CAP** and keep Vak XIII in view.
7. Engage an accountant at this point if any row in §8 is true (crypto settlement already is).

**Track A is the planned default while the half-time+ job holds.** It is still a full start-up, not a hobby exception and not a completed compliance state.

## 10. Track B — *zelfstandige in hoofdberoep*

Use this track if the employee job ends, drops below the hours test, or is never relied on.

### 2026 contributions (hoofdberoep)

Official legal base ([2026 table](https://socialsecurity.belgium.be/sites/default/files/content/docs/nl/sociaal-beleid-vorm-geven/zelfstandigen/bijdragennota_2026_tabel_nl.pdf)):

| | Legal (RSVZ, no fees) | Typical billed (Liantis / Partena, with fees) |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum / starter provisional per quarter | **€890.42** (on a notional **€17,374.08**) | **€925.59** (Liantis) / **€927.82** (Partena) |
| Primostarter reduced minimum / quarter | **€459.82** (on **€8,972.07**), first four quarters on request | **€477.98** (Liantis) / **€479.13** (Partena) |
| One-off primostarter discount on **first** quarter | **€118.52** | **€119.57** (Liantis) / **€123.50** (Partena, already subtracted in their starter quote) |
| Maximum / quarter | **€5,103.05** | **€5,304.62** (Liantis) |

**Primostarter:** no hoofdberoep / art. 37 / maxi-statute assisting spouse in the previous **five years**. Reduction is **on request** with a plausible income estimate. If actual income exceeds the estimate, the fund back-charges **plus** 3% quarterly and 7% one-off increases. Flanders note: [VLAIO — primostarter](https://www.vlaio.be/en/node/1635).

From year 4, provisionals are based on **N−3** income, then regularised on actual N.

**These minima are due even at €0 profit.** That is the cash-flow reason Track A is the default while the employee job qualifies.

### Rights that Track A does not give at low income

Health insurance as a self-employed person, statutory self-employed pension, *overbruggingsrecht*, maternity/paternity as a self-employed person, optional **VAPZ**. Budget for a recognised **mutualiteit** change when leaving the employee scheme.

### Tax

Same progressive rates as §8. Without an employee salary, the first slices of the self-employed profit sit in the lower bands — but the **social-contribution floor** still applies. Real expenses and paid contributions are deductible.

### Track B sequence

Same KBO / e604 / Peppol / USDC / CAP / accountant path as Track A, **plus**:

1. End or reduce the employee job only **after** the fund has coded the start quarter as hoofdberoep (avoid a gap in health cover).
2. Decide primostarter **in writing** at affiliation.
3. Set aside ~**€3,700–€3,800** for the first year’s minima (four billed quarters) unless a reduced primostarter request is accepted.
4. Recalculate the VAT exemption **pro rata** if the start date is not 1 January.

## 11. Choosing A vs B (decision, not a conclusion)

| Question | If yes → |
|---|---|
| Employee job still ≥ 50% and ≥ 235 h/quarter, contract allows a side activity? | **Stay on A** |
| Planning to drop the job within the year? | Start **A**, file a **statute change** to B on the quarter the hours fail (do not wait for the fund to notice). |
| Need self-employed social rights more than you need a low contribution bill? | **B** |
| Expected 2026 net profit from the agency work well under €17k, job kept? | **A** (B would still bill the minimum on empty profit) |
| Hours already below the test? | **B only** — A would be a mis-coding |

Neither track is “already done.” Both start with KBO + fund + e604.
