Handoff to a real Access Point

Screens differ per provider. This is the order of work at their product, not a click-path we run for you. We click nothing. We send nothing on Peppol.

Until a certified Access Point delivers a document on the Peppol network, you do not have a structured Belgian B2B e-invoice from this pack. The HTML and SAMPLE UBL here do not count. We are not an Access Point. This checklist is not a compliance certificate.

Give the Access Point (from official records only)

  • Legal name matching KBO public search — not a trading name you invented
  • KBO/BCE: 10 digits, no dots, for Peppol scheme 0208 — pasted from KBO, not guessed
  • VAT number as issued — leave blank in our intake until you have it
  • Registered address as in KBO
  • IBAN they want on invoices (their bank, not ours)
  • Who is authorised to onboard (their process: identity check is theirs)
  • Whether they need send, receive, or both
  • Current invoicing/accounting software name
  • Buyer list with official KBO values only, plus Directory lookup notes they made themselves

What they do at the Access Point (not here)

  1. Pick a certified AP from the live OpenPeppol list. Our shortlist is a 2026-08-26 snapshot of public BE APs, not a ranking.
  2. Create an account on that website. Complete their identity check. We do not run itsme, eID, or KYC.
  3. Ask them to register the participant as 0208: + 10-digit KBO. We cannot register a Peppol ID.
  4. If they want a test document, they may copy sample-ubl-invoice.xml and replace every REPLACE_WITH_* token. Do not transmit the file with tokens still in it. Do not invent VAT or KBO to make it “validate”.
  5. Look buyers up in the Peppol Directory as 0208: plus official KBO digits. The Directory does not prove they can send, only that a receive capability may be published.
  6. Send the first live invoice from the Access Point product. Keep their receipt / message id. A print of this pack is not a receipt.

What we did not do

  • No Access Point, no OpenPeppol membership, no SMP/SML, no live send/receive.
  • No Peppol ID registered for you or your clients.
  • No claim that you are compliant after opening these files.
  • No invented VAT, KBO, IBAN, or personal contact details in this pack.
  • No emails sent to buyers. No inboxes. No SMTP.
  • No bookkeeping, VAT advice, or identity onboarding.
