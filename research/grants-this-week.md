## Methodology

1. **Direct verification:** Each program's official page was visited directly — no third-party aggregators or summary sites were used.
2. **Currency check:** Programs were only included in the "Apply Now" list if the payout currency was explicitly stated (USD, USDC, or equivalent).
3. **Status check:** Programs with closed rounds, empty application pages, or $0 allocated funds were excluded from the apply-now list.
4. **Timeline check:** Programs whose decision or payout timeline extends beyond 2026-08-31 were noted but excluded from the apply-now list if no earlier payout was possible.
5. **No invented listings:** Only programs verified as open with a real application path are listed. Closed or unverifiable programs appear only in the "Excluded" section with their official URLs.
