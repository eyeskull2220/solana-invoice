## Use

1. Open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Fill **client**, **amount**, and **due date**. Optional: your name, invoice number, a note, and the Solana USDC address your client should pay.
3. Download the reminder file.
4. Send that file, or open it and copy the letter.

The downloaded file is self-contained and works offline.
