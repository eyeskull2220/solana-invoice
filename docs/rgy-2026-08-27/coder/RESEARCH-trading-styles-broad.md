## Simple first

People do not all trade the same movie.

A later invert seat that “steals a style” by **rewriting rungs, clock, or fees** is not stealing. That is a **new recipe**. This desk already named that class **H-** ([PR #211](https://github.com/eyeskull2220/solana-invoice/pull/211)): slower clock, wider spacing, maker. **Do not apply as invert. Do not open a slower-clock CODE PR.**

**Simple inequality (already GREEN in #211, restated so Coder does not re-argue it):**

```text
round-trip tax at 0.26% taker     typical 15m XRPEUR range
0.52%                          vs  ~0.45%
```

A closed invert pair must **bank more than 0.52%** or the round-trip is a fee factory. A typical 15-minute candle does not contain 0.52% of room. Days and weeks do. That is why **clock** is the first steal, and why putting a slower clock **into invert** is a hypothesis, not a patch.

**Seven families** (public record, then this desk):

1. Trend / breakout — follow the move, trail.  
2. Mean reversion / fade — buy the dip, sell the rip.  
3. Grid / market-make — many resting limits, maker, inventory.  
4. Fibonacci / swing rails — a **catalog** of levels, not 14 live orders.  
5. Invert / pair-swap — **two prices**, after fill **swap jobs**, **one** resting limit. A **style**. Not a new score.  
6. DCA / long-term hold — **separate book**. Do not mix into invert.  
7. Leverage / perps — **separate sleeve**. Do not mix.

Invert is family **5**. Families 1–4 are neighbors. Families 6–7 are **other rooms**. Mixing rooms is how a gate book becomes a different machine.

## 1. Trend / breakout — follow the move, trail

**What people do.** Wait until price makes a new high (or low), **join** that move, and **trail** until the move dies. Do not fade the first push. Classic public recipe: Turtle / Donchian — buy a 20-day (or 55-day) high, exit a 10-day (or 20-day) low, stop at ~2× ATR. ([TrendSpider Turtle](https://trendspider.com/learning-center/richard-dennis-turtle-trading-strategy/); [Turtle rules write-up](https://trendsandbreakouts.com/turtle-trading-rules); [Altrady crypto Turtle](https://www.altrady.com/blog/crypto-trading-strategies/turtle-trading-strategy-rules).)

Century-scale evidence is on **months**, not minutes: Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen, *A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing* — 1-month / 3-month / 12-month time-series momentum, 1880 onward, after costs. ([AQR](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/A-Century-of-Evidence-on-Trend-Following-Investing); [JPM 2017](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2017.44.1.015).)

| | Typical (public) | This desk |
|---|---|---|
| **Clock** | **1h–1d** for crypto adaptations. Original Turtles were **daily**. Public crypto ports warn: **do not go below 1h** — 15m/5m is noise plus fees. ([github turtle-strategy note](https://github.com/0xbhvn/turtle-strategy)) | Invert lock is **15m**. Putting Donchian on 15m XRPEUR as invert is **H-**. |
| **Typical hold** | Days to weeks. AQR rebalance is **monthly**. Honest 1d Donchian on Kraken printed **14 trades / 3 years**. | Invert 1-limit 2779 fills is **thousands** of clips — a different clock. |
| **Fee sensitivity** | **Low** if you trade rarely. Same 0.26% Kraken taker: **1d +4.33%** (14 trades, 1.3%/yr fees) vs **4h −6.01%** vs **1m −25% / zero wins**. Quote: on 1-minute bars “the fee is roughly six times the average move you’re trying to catch.” ([nar1-frames, DEV, 2026-08-21](https://dev.to/nar1frames/i-built-a-crypto-trading-bot-it-lost-to-doing-nothing-355a)) | 15m invert at 0.26% is the **4h-or-faster** side of that table. |
| **When it dies** | **Churn / chop.** False breakouts. Whipsaw. The Turtle filter *skips* the 20-day signal after a winner because big trends often start after failed chops. | Invert does **not** trail a 10-bar low. It sits a **fixed** opposite price. A trend that never tags the opposite is **inventory time**, not extra fills. |
| **Dies vs trend** | Trend is **food**. Chop is **poison**. | Invert’s 2779 FAIL is mostly **churn vs 0.52%**, not “missed the Turtle trail.” |

**STEAL (around invert — do not rewrite rungs):**

- Decision on a **closed** bar; fill later (`t` decide → `t+1` eligible). Same lie-detector as Freqtrade / CuteMarkets / [#199](https://github.com/eyeskull2220/solana-invoice/pull/199).
- **Confirmation** before joining a move (Turtle skip-after-win; AQR lookback of **months**).
- **Fee × timeframe** table: slower clocks survive 0.26%; faster clocks do not.
- Stay **out** most of the time (that study ~80% flat). Flat is not a fill toward 8.

**HYPOTHESIS (would change invert — not applied):** replace fib pair with Donchian 20/55; trail instead of a fixed TP; move invert’s decision clock to 1h/4h/1d (**PR 211 H-3**). That is a **new book**. Not CODE. Not a reseal. Not a D6 pass.

## 2. Mean reversion / fade — buy the dip, sell the rip

**What people do.** Price stretches, then snaps back. Buy when it is “too cheap,” sell when it is “too rich.” Tools: **RSI** (Wilder 1978; typical 14-period, oversold ~30, overbought ~70) and **Bollinger Bands** (20-period mean ± 2σ; buy lower band, exit the middle). ([RSI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_strength_index); [Bollinger Bands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bollinger_Bands); [Bollinger’s site](https://www.bollingerbands.com/); practitioner fade write-up [CoinSeekly](https://coinseekly.com/learn/mean-reversion-crypto-guide); bot recipe [Block Research](https://blockresearch.ai/blog/mean-reversion-trading-bot-guide).)

| | Typical (public) | This desk |
|---|---|---|
| **Clock** | Retail crypto often **15m–1h**. Academic 15m reversal is **real as a sign**, dead as a **size**. | Invert 15m is already this **horizon**, not this **trigger**. Invert does not wait for RSI 30. |
| **Typical hold** | Minutes to a few hours — back to the mean, not a new trend. Coinquant scaled a 5% **daily** stretch to **~0.6% on 15m**. | 1-limit mean hold on the **two-clip** sibling was 47.6 bars (~12 h) / median 13 bars (~3.25 h). Median 13 is the **churn** clock. |
| **Fee sensitivity** | **Extreme.** Neklyudov & Wengrowicz 2026: 15m sign-reversion on 183 Binance pairs; gross edge peaks **~1.3 bp** vs cheapest spot RT **5 bp** and taker **10–20 bp**. **Not one of 183 pairs** clears 5 bp. ([arXiv:2608.21888](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.21888)) Coinquant 78 backtests, fees on: **15m avg −14.4%**; one 15m Bollinger run **693 trades, >$2,300 fees on $10,000**. ([Coinquant 2026-08-03](https://www.coinquant.ai/blog/building-a-mean-reversion-strategy-in-cryptocurrency-markets-evidence-from-78-backtests)) | Desk primary RT is **52 bp** — ~**40×** that 1.3 bp peak. 2779 × 0.52 EUR ≈ **1445 EUR** if clip 200. Same mill. |
| **When it dies** | **Trend.** The mean walks. Lower-band buys in a bear are a falling knife. Coinquant: same logic **+16.3% bull / −40.6% bear**. High win rate ≠ profit. | Invert **is** a fade between two prices. A one-way month leaves the opposite sitting (2023 slices, [#194](https://github.com/eyeskull2220/solana-invoice/pull/194)). That is **stuck inventory**, not extra RSI signals. |

**STEAL:**

- **Sign can be real and still not pay the fee.** Steal that sentence. Do not steal “add RSI so 15m pays 0.26%.”
- Stretch vs **round-trip tax** before arming. 0.6% 15m threshold vs 0.52% RT is a **thin** margin; vs 0.80/1.60 shadows it is negative **by construction**.
- Regime: do not fade a **named** trend day as if it were a dip (Torres 13 Jul 2023 is a **skip / path** tape, not a Bollinger long).

**HYPOTHESIS (not applied):** require RSI&lt;30 / lower-band touch before the invert buy; scale 15m stretch to 0.6%; close at the 20-SMA instead of the paired fib. New trigger = new recipe.

## 3. Grid / market-make — many resting limits, maker, inventory

**What people do.** Post **many** buys below and sells above, harvest the oscillation, keep inventory from running off one side. Maker (resting) is the intended fee. Grid spacing must **clear round-trip fees** or “grid profit” is a lie. Hummingbot `GridExecutor`: each level is its own mini state machine `OPEN_ORDER_PLACED → OPEN_ORDER_FILLED → CLOSE_ORDER_PLACED → COMPLETE`. **N executors ⇒ N concurrent opens.** 1-limit is **N = 1**. ([GridExecutor](https://hummingbot.org/strategies/v2-strategies/executors/gridexecutor/); [Pure market making](https://hummingbot.org/strategies/v1-strategies/pure-market-making/); [Ping-pong](https://hummingbot.org/strategies/v1-strategies/strategy-configs/ping-pong/).)

Practitioner spacing: often **0.5–1%** of price to absorb maker/taker; below **~0.2%** the whole cycle is fees. ([Neutralis — grids, fees, risks](https://neutralis.finance/insights/crypto-market-making-bot-grids); [VoiceOfChain grid profit](https://voiceofchain.com/academy/grid-bot-profit-calculation); [cryptogates.io backtest mistakes](https://cryptogates.io/common-grid-trading-mistakes-in-crypto-backtests/).)

| | Typical (public) | This desk |
|---|---|---|
| **Clock** | Every **touch** of a live level. Faster tape ⇒ more cycles **if** N is large. | Invert **forbids** N = 14 live pairs. Fill-every-rung (`2bfb1b68`) **is** a grid of fibs. Retired. |
| **Typical hold** | Minutes–hours per **completed** level; **days–weeks** of leftover inventory in a trend. | 2779 at ~2 fills/day is closer to **one** ping-pong than to a 10-level grid. |
| **Fee sensitivity** | **The product.** Net per cycle ≈ `gap − 2×fee`. If `gap < 2×fee`, every completed cycle **loses**. Maker is how grids **try** to shrink `2×fee`. | Paper engine **0.26% taker** primary. Live Kraken Pro **Tier 1 since 2026-07-09** is **0.40% maker / 0.80% taker** — not a maker rebate. ([Fee schedule](https://www.kraken.com/features/fee-schedule); [cross-platform tiers](https://support.kraken.com/articles/cross-platform-fee-tier-changes)) Switching invert to “maker because grids make” is **PR 200 H6 / PR 211 H-1**. **Not applied.** |
| **When it dies** | **Trend / inventory drift.** Downtrend: keep buying, bag the asset. Uptrend: sell the inventory, miss the rally. Public: ~95% of bots lose in 90 days is a marketing-adjacent claim; the **mechanism** (inventory skew) is the useful part. ([Neutralis inventory](https://neutralis.finance/insights/market-making-risk-inventory); [Gate.io 6-month grid](https://gridlens-geo-blog.vercel.app/01-gate-grid-6-months.html); [DEV inventory skew guard](https://dev.to/jmolinasoler/adding-trade-guards-to-a-grid-bot-gas-ratio-price-impact-stop-loss-and-inventory-skew-12db)) | 9097 grind = grid death by **count**. 7923 two-clip = grid death by **count** with clip 200. 2779 1-limit = **less count, still FAIL**. Trend months are the **other** death (opposite never prints). |

**STEAL:**

- Fill count scales with **how many levels are working**, not with Fibonacci labels ([#212](https://github.com/eyeskull2220/solana-invoice/pull/212)).
- **Spacing ≥ 2× fee** is an **identity**, not a WFO of α. At 0.26%, do not arm a pair whose `(Hi−Lo)/Lo < 0.52%`.
- **N = 1** ping-pong (Hummingbot: after a fill, only the **opposite** side) is the **shape** of invert, not a grid of 14.
- Inventory cap / skip extra rungs — already locked as EUR 200 and one rest. Do not “improve” it by restoring the ladder.

**HYPOTHESIS (not applied):** rest 3/3 or 14 fibs on `invert-paper`; post-only maker as **primary** fee; trailing grid rebuild. Desk already has a **separate** `grid-paper` track (journal track 3). **Do not copy a grid init onto `dca-paper` or onto the gate book.**

## 4. Fibonacci / swing rails — catalog, not 14 live orders

**What people do.** Draw a swing. Mark retracements (0.236 / 0.382 / 0.5 / 0.618 / 0.786) and extensions (1.272 / 1.618 / …). Use those lines as a **map**. Wait for **confirmation** at a zone. Extensions are **targets**, not extra entries. ([Ticker Daily swing fib](https://tickerdaily.com/learn/swing-trading/fibonacci); [proplynq: tag ≠ entry](https://proplynq.com/blog/fibonacci-retracement-levels-how-to-actually-trade-them).)

Academic cold water: Tsinaslanidis, Guijarro & Voukelatos (ESWA 2022) — bounce probability on Fibonacci zones is **statistically indistinguishable** from non-Fibonacci zones; a Fibonacci trading rule does **not** beat random levels. ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957417421012495); [open PDF](https://riunet.upv.es/server/api/core/bitstreams/07add8c4-4239-44e7-bf1f-93d66108918d/content).)

## 5. Invert / pair-swap — two prices, after fill swap jobs, one resting limit

**What this style is (plain language).** Pick **exactly two** prices on **one** spot pair. Rest **one** limit. When it fills, those two prices **swap jobs**: the filled price is not re-armed until the **opposite** fills. Spot: no naked short. After a sell you are cash; next rest is a buy-back.

## 6. DCA / long-term hold — separate book; do not mix

**What people do.** Buy a **fixed cash amount** on a **calendar** (week / month), ignore the tape, hold years. Goal is an average cost, not a round-trip. Kraken’s own primer: top surveyed crypto strategy (59%); “set and forget”; **not** for people trying to harvest 15m. Higher-frequency DCA still pays more fees, but **orders of magnitude** fewer than a grid. ([Kraken Learn — DCA](https://www.kraken.com/learn/finance/dollar-cost-averaging).)

Public split vs grid: DCA is the **uptrend / years** tool; grid is the **range / days** tool. Mixing them in one bot is a common blog tip and a **bad desk move**. ([Panshi DCA vs grid](https://blog.panshi.io/en/posts/btc-dca-vs-grid/); [Cryptorobot.ai comparison](https://cryptorobot.ai/blog/dca-vs-grid-trading-bots-comparison); [unCoded: separate keys, neither system should influence the other](https://uncoded.ch/blogs/dca-bot-vs-grid-bot-vs-hft-bot-which-strategy-works-when).)

| | Typical (public) | This desk |
|---|---|---|
| **Clock** | **Weekly / monthly.** Not 15m. | `dca-paper`: five BTCUSD slices, **already filled**, held. |
