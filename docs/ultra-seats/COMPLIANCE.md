## 2. Bijberoep vs hoofdberoep

RSVZ (fetched **2026-08-27**): bijberoep is available when, at the same time as the self-employed activity, the person is an employee/civil servant (or certain replacement-income / pension-rights cases). Same **legal start-up duties** as hoofdberoep (KBO, fund, VAT identification). The difference is the **contribution floor**.

| | **Bijberoep** | **Hoofdberoep** |
|---|---|---|
| **Hours test (employee)** | Main job at least **half** of a full-time job in that firm/sector. RSVZ: hours per month ≥ half of a full-time worker in the same enterprise or sector. Statutory teacher: **6/10** of a full timetable (contract teacher: half). | Hours test fails, job ends, or is never relied on. |
| **Minimum contribution** | **None** below the bijberoep floor. | **Always** a minimum, even at €0 profit. |
| **2026 legal figures (RSVZ)** | No contributions if net taxable self-employed income **< €1,922.16**. From that floor to **€17,374.08**: reduced scale. At/above **€17,374.08**: same as hoofdberoep. | 20.50% on income up to **€75,024.54**; 14.16% on the slice up to **€110,562.42**. Minimum calculated on **€17,374.08**. |
| **Social rights at low income** | Stay with the **employee** scheme. Low bijberoep contributions do not build a self-employed pension. | Built from self-employed contributions (pension, sickness, bridging right, etc.). |
| **Default while a qualifying job is kept** | Track bijberoep — still a full start-up, not a hobby exception. | Only after the hours test fails or the job is dropped. |

**Official URLs (fetched 2026-08-27):**

- [RSVZ — Wat betekent dat juist: zelfstandige in bijberoep?](https://www.rsvz.be/nl/wat-betekent-dat-juist-zelfstandige-bijberoep)
- [RSVZ — Zelfstandige in bijberoep](https://www.rsvz.be/nl/zelfstandige-bijberoep) (same-duties sentence; 2026 floors €1,922.16 / €17,374.08)

## 4. Vrijstelling €25,000 still needs a BTW-ID

Small-business VAT exemption (art. 56bis VAT Code) does **not** mean “no VAT number.” The person remains a taxable person, **identified**, who does not charge VAT and cannot deduct input VAT.

FPS e-invoicing hub (fetched **2026-08-27**): from **1 January 2026**, Belgian VAT-taxable businesses must use structured e-invoices between each other. **“Hebt u een actief btw-nummer? Dan is deze verplichting ook op uw onderneming van toepassing.”** The same page states the mandate **includes** the vrijstellingsregeling when annual turnover is not more than **€25,000**.

FPS Finance listing guidelines 2026 (PDF, official) still treat kleine ondernemingen under the **€25,000** Belgian-turnover line and still require a **BE + 10-digit** identification number on the customer listing.

**What the exemption does *not* remove**

- VAT identification (KBO number activated by FPS Finance — typically e604A / MyMinfin).
- Invoice rules (mention *Bijzondere vrijstellingsregeling kleine ondernemingen* — **after** a real number exists; until then **do not FACTUUR**).
- Annual customer listing via Intervat (including nihil).
- Peppol **receive** capability; Peppol **send** to Belgian VAT-registered B2B customers.

Turnover for the ceiling is the **EUR** value of supplies, including USDC-paid jobs (§5). Crypto is not a way around €25,000.

## 5. USDC received for work is EUR *beroepsinkomen*

Payment for agent-delivered services is **professional income** (*beroepsinkomen*), not private-asset capital gains, not miscellaneous speculation, and not “deeleconomie.” Holding the token does not defer the fee.

**Statutory hook (form of payment does not matter):** WIB 92 art. 23 treats as professional income the profits, proceeds, and remunerations from a professional activity. Art. 24 (winsten) and the in-kind rules sit on the same idea: the taxable amount is the EUR value when the consideration is received.

**Justel warning:** the coordinated WIB 92 on Justel is **not updated past ~2010**. Current text lives on Fisconetplus. Cite Justel only as the code identity, then confirm on Fisconetplus / with the accountant.

**Booking plan (for the accountant — this seat does not book):**

1. Do **not** raise a FACTUUR until KBO + VAT identification exist. Until then: no stamp.
2. On-chain receipt date = income date. Convert USDC → **EUR at that moment**.
3. There is **no** FPS-published official crypto FX table. A defensible professional method (accountant to confirm): ECB USD/EUR reference rate of the receipt date (last TARGET day if weekend), document 1 USDC ≈ 1 USD and any deviation.
4. Later cash-out to a Belgian EUR account is a **second** event (FX difference), not a chance to leave the fee unreported.

## 8. GBA cookies — no banner if zero trackers

GBA (last update **17/11/2023**, fetched **2026-08-27**): consent is required for placing/reading cookies and other trackers **unless** the site only uses **strictly necessary** cookies. Analytics cookies are **not** strictly necessary in Belgian GBA guidance, including first-party.

**If the site places/reads zero cookies and zero other trackers** (no analytics, no pixels, no local-storage beacons, no third-party embeds that set storage): there is nothing to consent to. **Do not add a banner for theatre.** A banner that asks for consent the site never uses is noise; a banner that is added “just in case” often arrives together with a tracker.

Still give **clear information** (privacy page, §11) that no non-necessary cookies/trackers are used. If a tracker is added later, GBA rules for a valid banner apply (prior, free, specific, informed, active; accept **and** refuse on the same layer; no cookie wall).

Static HTML in this repo (`index.html`, `catalog.html`, `solana-invoice.html`) has **no** `gtag`, analytics, or pixel strings on **2026-08-27**. Hosting platforms (Surge, GitHub Pages, browsers) may set **their own** cookies — that is the host’s processing, not a reason for this seat to stamp a banner. Re-audit before adding a form, a paywall, or a third-party script.

## 9. B2B `info@` + opt-out — WER XII.13

**Rule (opt-in default):** WER art. XII.13 §1 — use of electronic mail for advertising is forbidden without the addressee’s prior, free, specific, and informed consent. §2: every advertising message must (1) explain the right to object to future advertising and (2) provide an electronic means to exercise that right. §4: the sender proves consent.

**B2B impersonal exception** is **not** in XII.13 itself. It is in **art. 1, 2°** of the Royal Decree of **4 April 2003** (Belgisch Staatsblad **28 May 2003**, numac `2003011238`), which exempts prior consent for messages to **legal persons** when the electronic contact details used are **impersonal**. The report to the King lists `info@…`, `contact@…`, `privacy@…`, `sales@…`, `bestelling@…`, `klantendienst@…`. Named mailboxes (`voornaam.naam@bedrijf.be`) are **natural persons** — opt-in required.

**Even on the exception:** every message still needs a working **opt-out** (XII.13 §2 + KB art. 2). Offers must be aimed at the **legal person**, not at the humans behind it. Eenmanszaak / natural-person traders are **not** this exception.

FPS Economy “Spam in 23 vragen” (official PDF) restates the same split and quotes XII.13.

**This seat:** STARTABLE = draft the Scout line (§12). **BLOCKED** = sending CS/commercial mail.

- [Justel / Staatsblad — KB 4 april 2003, numac 2003011238](https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/eli/besluit/2003/04/04/2003011238/staatsblad) — fetched 2026-08-27 (ELI staatsblad; pub. 28/05/2003)
