## Could a listing be started for free with no login?

**No. ZERO.**

Both sites advertise a free vendor listing. Both require a person, an email, and a vendor account before a product page can go live. That is not a no-login product form.

### GetApp

GetApp does not have its own submit page. The homepage footer “Get listed” goes to G2 Digital Markets (GetApp, Capterra, and Software Advice share this cabinet).

| Step | URL | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing site | https://www.g2digitalmarkets.com/ | “Get Listed” and “Get Your Product Listed” |
| Start form | https://app.g2digitalmarkets.com/get-listed/start | **Business Email**, Product Name, Product Website. Continue stays off until those are filled. |
| Vendor login | https://app.g2digitalmarkets.com/login | Email + password cabinet |

The start form is public, but it is **account signup**, not a product-only drop box. On-page copy on 2026-08-26 said:

- This flow is for listing one product **and gaining access to a G2 Digital Markets account**.
- If approved, the first listing is published on **Capterra**. GetApp and Software Advice are added **later**, after that account exists.

Required **Business Email** is PII. Filling it would also create a login. That fails the no-login rule and the PII scan. Not submitted.

Homepage also has **Join or Log in** for buyers. That is a separate login wall, not a product form.

### Crozdesk

Crozdesk homepage “Apply to list your software” and the claims-page “Create a NEW Software Listing” both go to the same Revleads vendor application:

https://vendor.revleads.com/user/signup

Required fields on 2026-08-26:

- First name, last name, email, phone (PII)
- Company name, headquarters country
- Product name, software URL, category, short description
- Monthly marketing budget

On-page copy said: if accepted, they **email login details** so the vendor can finish the listing in a portal. That is an account application, then a login, then a listing. Not a no-login product form. Not submitted.

Claim form (only if a listing already exists): https://crozdesk.com/claims/new

That form also asks for first name, last name, country, company, position, email, and phone. There is no matching listing to claim. Not submitted.

Header **Log in** / **Sign up** on Crozdesk is a site account, not a product form.
