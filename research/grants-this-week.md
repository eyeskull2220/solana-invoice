# Grants & RFP Research — Week of 2026-08-25

## Methodology

All programs below were checked live on **2026-08-25** against their official public pages. For each entry we verified:

1. **Status** — Is the program currently accepting applications right now (not just announced)?
2. **Funding currency & amount** — Is the payout currency clearly stated? Is the amount specified?
3. **Timeline** — Is there a defined or rolling deadline that is currently open?
4. **Eligibility clarity** — Are the eligibility requirements publicly documented?

A program is listed under **Apply Now** only if all four criteria are confirmed. Programs that fail any criterion are listed under **Checked & Excluded** with the specific reason.

No listings are invented or extrapolated from past rounds. If a page was ambiguous or unverifiable on the check date, it was excluded.
